I am an Assistant Professor in the [Research Division on Supercomputing Systems](https://www.cc.tohoku.ac.jp/english/member/rd/) at the Cyberscience Center, Tohoku University, and a member of the [High Performance Computing Laboratory](https://www.hpc.is.tohoku.ac.jp/home-en/member-en/). I received the Doctor of Engineering degree from the University of Toyama in 2025 after completing research on artificial intelligence systems for data analysis and optimization.

My research asks how intelligent algorithms and modern computing systems can be co-designed so that learning and optimization remain efficient, scalable, and useful on real scientific and engineering workloads. I work at the intersection of _Artificial Intelligence_, _High-Performance Computing_, _Evolutionary Optimization_, and _Brain-Inspired Neural Networks_, with representative applications in AI for Science, energy and weather, human–robot collaboration, healthcare, and social simulation.

My academic service includes journal and conference reviewing across computational intelligence, systems engineering, machine learning, and optimization. I have also served in editorial-support roles, as Track Chair for the cognitive computing and brain-derived algorithms track of IEEE MCSoC 2026, and as a Program Committee member for AgenticAI4HPC'26 at SC26. A direct list is available on the [services page]({{ '/services/' | relative_url }}).

Recent recognition includes first-place results across all four numerical optimization tracks at IEEE CEC 2025 and IEEE CEC 2026, the SWoPP 2026 Best Research Award for co-authored work, a silver award at IEEE WCCI 2024, and research fellowships from the Japan Science and Technology Agency. The complete evidence-linked record is listed under [awards]({{ '/awards/' | relative_url }}).
